Niels Bohr (1913) - Atomic Model

$$mvr = \frac{nh}{2\pi} \quad | \quad E_n = -13.6 \frac{Z^2}{n^2} \text{ eV}$$
Henry Moseley (1913) - Modern Periodic Law
